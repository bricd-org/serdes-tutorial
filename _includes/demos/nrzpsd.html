Three things to try

  1. Find where the power is. At f = fN the PSD is only 3.9 dB below DC and 77.4 % of the power lies below 2.5 GHz. That is why channel loss is quoted at Nyquist.
  2. Move to the first null. Click f = fb: the PSD has a null at 5 GHz with 90.3 % of the power below it. At 1.5 fb the second lobe is 13.5 dB down and 93.1 % is below.
  3. Change the bit rate. Set 10 Gb/s: fN moves to 5 GHz, but the readouts at f = fN stay at −3.9 dB and 77.4 %. The spectrum depends only on f·Tb.
