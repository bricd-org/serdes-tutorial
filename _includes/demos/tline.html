Three things to try

  1. Read the default. 10 mm of FR-4 (εr 4) and a 50 ps edge: v = 1.5×108 m/s, 6.67 ps/mm. Anything longer than trv/6 = 1.25 mm is a transmission line, and this trace is one (Worked Example 3.1).
  2. Slow the edge. Set the rise time to 500 ps: the limit grows to 12.5 mm and the same 10 mm trace becomes lumped. The edge, not the bit rate, decides.
  3. Go on-chip. Set 1 mm: with a 50 ps edge it is lumped. Sharpen the edge to 20 ps and even 1 mm is a line (limit 0.50 mm). A 50 Ω line has 334 nH and 133 pF per metre.
