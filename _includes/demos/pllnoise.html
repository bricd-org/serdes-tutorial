Three things to try

  1. Read 3 MHz. At fu = 3 MHz the total is 1.00 ps rms: 0.69 ps from the VCO and 0.72 ps from the in-band floor, right at the optimum (2.9 MHz).
  2. Move the bandwidth. At 1 MHz the VCO dominates (1.20 of 1.27 ps); at 6 MHz the floor dominates (1.02 of 1.13 ps).
  3. Improve the VCO. Set the VCO to −110 dBc/Hz at 1 MHz: the optimum drops to 1.0 MHz with 0.59 ps there, and 3 MHz now gives 0.76 ps.
