Three things to try

  1. The book's case. σ = 0.02 UI (4 ps at 5 Gb/s): the eye stays open over 0.72 UI at 10−12.
  2. Double the jitter. Click 0.04 UI (8 ps): the opening shrinks to 0.45 UI, because each Gaussian tail is about 7σ wide at 10−12. At 0.08 UI the bathtub closes: even the centre reaches 2.1e−10.
  3. Change the transition density. Drag it from 0.5 to 0.1 or to 1: the opening barely moves (0.73 or 0.72 UI). The density scales the BER, but the tails are so steep that the width hardly changes.
