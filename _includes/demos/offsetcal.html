Three things to try

  1. Read the default. A 5-bit DAC over ±3σ (±14.7 mV) gives a 0.92 mV LSB. Raw, 70 % of samplers meet ±5.1 mV but only 24 % of 4-sampler receivers; trimmed, 99.99 % and 99.96 %.
  2. Use fewer bits. With 3 bits the LSB grows to 3.68 mV, still inside the ±5.1 mV spec: 99.92 % of receivers pass.
  3. Narrow the range. Set ±1.5σ with 5 bits: the LSB halves to 0.46 mV, but samplers beyond the range cannot be trimmed and receivers drop to 95 %.
