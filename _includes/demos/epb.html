Three things to try

  1. Read the default. 50 mW at 5 Gb/s is 10.0 pJ for every bit, 160 pJ for each 16-bit word.
  2. Load the Open-SERDES budget. Drag the power to about 215 mW (the first-cut budget of Chapter 14 is 216 mW) and keep 5 Gb/s: about 43 pJ/b.
  3. Compare with the Purdue OpenSerDes. Set about 438 mW at 2 Gb/s: about 219 pJ/b, five times the energy per bit of the Open-SERDES first cut.
