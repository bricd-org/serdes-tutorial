Three things to try

  1. Read the default. With FO4 = 42 ps the clock tree tops out near 2.98 GHz, 1/(8·FO4). A 5 Gb/s UI is only 4.8 FO4, and a half-rate stage clears the target by 19 %.
  2. Go to the slow corner. Click 55 ps: the limit drops to 2.27 GHz and half rate misses by 9 %. That is why the last serializer stages of Open-SERDES are HBT CML, not CMOS.
  3. Aim for 10 Gb/s. Back at 42 ps, set 10 Gb/s: a UI is 2.4 FO4 and half rate falls 40 % short.
