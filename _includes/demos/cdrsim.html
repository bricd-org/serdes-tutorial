Three things to try

  1. Run the baseline. +200 ppm, KP = 1/8 LSB, KP/KI = 128: locks in 0.15 µs and tracks with 2.5 ps rms (14 ps pp); the integral register settles at 202 ppm.
  2. Use an integer KP. Click integer KP = 1 LSB: it locks in 0.02 µs, but the tracking error grows to 10.1 ps rms (50 ps pp).
  3. Push the offset. With the baseline, +500 ppm still locks (0.25 µs) with the register at 500 ppm, beyond the 488 ppm that KP alone can slew. The integral path carries the frequency.
