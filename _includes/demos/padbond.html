Three things to try

  1. Read the default. At 5 Gb/s a 1 mm wire alone gives 16.2 dB of return loss at fN and the 0.63 pF pad alone 12.4 dB, but both together 18.5 dB: the wire partly tunes out the pad. The 10 dB limits are 2.12 mm and 0.85 pF.
  2. Lengthen the wire. At 2.5 mm the wire alone gives 8.7 dB, below the 10 dB goal (Worked Example 14.2), yet with the pad the pair still reaches 12.5 dB. At 1.00 pF, √(L/C) = 50 Ω, the artificial line of Chapter 3 (10.6 dB).
  3. Go to 10 Gb/s. Click 10 Gb/s: at 5 GHz the 1 mm wire gives 10.5 dB and the pad only 7.1 dB. The limits halve to 1.06 mm and 0.42 pF, which is why the 10 Gb/s target needs a T-coil.
