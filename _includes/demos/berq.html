Q = EH/2σn –SNR –BER –one error every –

Three things to try

  1. Hit 10−12. The defaults, EH 100 mV and σn 7.11 mV, give Q = 7.03, SNR 16.9 dB and a BER of 1.0e−12: one error every 3.28 min at 5 Gb/s, and every 1.64 min at 10 Gb/s.
  2. Lose or win 1 dB. Raise σn to 7.98 mV (SNR 15.9 dB): 1.9e−10, an error every 1.08 s. Lower it to 6.34 mV (17.9 dB): 1.6e−15, one error every 1.49 days.
  3. Halve the eye. Set EH to 50 mV: Q 3.52, BER 2.2e−4, an error every 914 ns.
