  1. Start with nothing on. The main cursor gives 170 mV against 24 mV of pre-cursor, 136 mV in the first two post-cursors and 130 mV in the tail: the PDA eye is −242 mV.
  2. Add the DFE, then the CTLE. The 2-tap DFE removes the first posts and the eye opens to +31 mV. CTLE 8 dB then cuts the tail from 130 to 45 mV (the main cursor drops to 92 mV): 78 mV.
