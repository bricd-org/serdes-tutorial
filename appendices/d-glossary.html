Appendix DGlossary

bang-bang phase detector that compares a data, an edge and the next data sample

time window over which a sampler effectively averages its input

BER against sampling position across the UI

bit-error rate: errors divided by bits; Open-SERDES targets \(10^{-12}\)

process with both bipolar (HBT) and CMOS transistors, such as SG13G2

collector-emitter breakdown voltage of an HBT with the base open

clock and data recovery: extracts the sampling phase from the data transitions

current-mode logic: differential pairs steering a constant tail current into resistors

layout that interleaves matched devices so gradients cancel

continuous-time linear equalizer: an analog high-frequency boost

sample of the pulse response at an integer number of UIs from the main sample

duty-cycle distortion: unequal high and low times, a two-valued DJ

TX FFE setting that lowers repeated bits instead of raising transitions

decision-feedback equalizer: subtracts ISI predicted from past decisions

deterministic (bounded) and random (Gaussian) jitter

jitter model with DJ as two impulses convolved with Gaussian RJ

electrostatic discharge, and the diodes that protect the pads from it

overlay of the received waveform folded at one or two UIs

feed-forward equalizer: an FIR filter, here in the TX driver

heterojunction bipolar transistor; SG13G2 npn13G2 with \(f_T\approx300\) GHz

attenuation through the channel, \(-20\log_{10}\lvert S_{21}\rvert\)

inter-symbol interference: energy of a bit that spills into its neighbors

jitter tolerance: largest sinusoidal jitter, against frequency, that still meets the BER

internal path from the TX output to the RX input for self-test

layout-versus-schematic and design-rule checks before tape-out

a latch input so small that the decision is late or undefined

simulation repeated with random device mismatch

two-level and four-level pulse-amplitude modulation

half the symbol rate: 2.5 GHz for 5 Gb/s NRZ

peak-distortion analysis: worst-case eye from the sum of absolute cursors

circuit that mixes two clock phases to make any phase between them

single-sideband noise power near a clock, \(\mathcal{L}(f)\), in dBc/Hz

phase-locked loop: multiplies a clean reference up to the VCO frequency

RC network that splits a differential clock into I and Q

pseudo-random binary sequence from a linear-feedback shift register

channel output for one isolated 1-UI pulse

reflection at a port, \(-20\log_{10}\lvert S_{11}\rvert\)

frequency-domain description of a network by incident and reflected waves

clocked comparator (latch) that decides each bit

multiplexer tree that turns a parallel word into a serial stream

source-series-terminated (voltage-mode) driver

eye at a target BER computed from the ISI and noise distributions

coupled-inductor network that hides pad capacitance from the line

unit interval: one bit time, 200 ps

equalizer that forces selected cursors to zero, ignoring noise
